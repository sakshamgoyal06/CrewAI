How it works

A plan in two minutes. A coach every day after.

1

Set a goal and a date

Weight, body fat or muscle, and a deadline. Magnus works out the targets from your body and week, and tells you straight if the date needs an unsafe pace.

2

Log the way you talk

Type “paneer bhurji and 2 parathas”, snap the plate, or scan a packet. Every estimate is a card you can fix before it counts.

3

Stay on pace

Steps and weigh-ins sync from Health Connect, workouts from Hevy. Every week you see where you are, and the one thing to fix next week.

Why it's different

Trackers count. Magnus coaches.

Targets that learn

Your calorie target starts from a formula, then adjusts to what your real intake and weight trend say your maintenance is.

Indian food, first

Rotis, dals, sabzis, thalis and street food are in the database with sensible portions, not guessed from American recipes.

One number to watch

On-track days this week. Five of seven is the target, and a day only counts when meals, protein, calories, steps and training line up.

It notices when you slip

Two off days in a row, and it looks for the cause in your own data and suggests one small experiment, then checks if it helped.
